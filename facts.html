🔭 Space Facts

Little pieces of knowledge from our enormous universe.

☀️ The Sun is a Star

The Sun is the star at the center of our Solar System. Its energy makes life on Earth possible.

🌍 Earth is Moving

Earth rotates on its axis while also traveling around the Sun.

🌙 The Moon is Earth's Natural Satellite

The Moon orbits Earth and strongly influences ocean tides.

💡 Light is Extremely Fast

Light travels through empty space at about 300,000 kilometers per second.

🌌 The Universe is Huge

Astronomers observe enormous numbers of galaxies, each containing huge numbers of stars.

🕳️ Black Holes Have Powerful Gravity

A black hole is a region of spacetime where gravity is so strong that light cannot escape from within its event horizon.
